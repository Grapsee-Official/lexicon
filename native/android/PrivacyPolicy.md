## 1. Who we are

Lexicon ("we", "us", "our") publishes the Lexicon Android application
("Lexicon", "the app"). Lexicon is an offline-first English dictionary and
word-exploration tool. The app's core engine — a C++ hash table + DAWG
(Directed Acyclic Word Graph) implementation — runs entirely on your
device. The cloud sync service that delivers optional language-pack updates
is operated by Lexicon and is documented in §3 below.

---

## 2. What we collect — and why

Lexicon is built around a hard design rule: **your words stay on your
device**. We do not collect your search history, your saved words, your
collections, your voice recordings, or the photographs you point at the
image-search camera. The only data that ever leaves your device is the
minimum needed to keep the dictionary up to date and to diagnose crashes.

### 2.1 Data that *does* leave the device

| Data type                   | Purpose                                            | Linked to you? | Encrypted in transit? |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|----------------|-----------------------|
| **Crash logs**              | Diagnose native-code crashes in the C++ engine.    | No             | Yes (HTTPS/TLS 1.2+)  |
| **Sync metadata**           | Fetch word-list delta updates from the cloud.      | No             | Yes (HTTPS/TLS 1.2+)  |
| **Integrity-check result**  | Detect corrupt local SQLite database installs.     | No             | Yes (HTTPS/TLS 1.2+)  |

#### 2.1.1 Crash logs

If the app crashes, an anonymised crash report is sent to our crash
reporting service. The report contains:

* The Java/Kotlin stack trace (class + method names from the Compose UI,
  the ViewModel, and the engine JNI bridge — see
  `proguard-rules.pro §6` which keeps source file names and line numbers
  in release builds).
* The native stack trace from `liblexicon-engine.so` (symbolicated against
  our build-server debug symbols; the symbols themselves are NOT shipped
  to your device).
* The Android OS version, device model, and ABI (`arm64-v8a` or
  `x86_64`).
* The app version name and version code (`1.0.0` / `1`).
* The crash timestamp.

The report does **not** contain:

* Your search queries, saved words, or collections.
* Your voice recordings or photographs.
* Your advertising identifier, Android ID, or IMEI.
* Your phone number, email, contacts, or location.

#### 2.1.2 Sync metadata

If you have **opted in** to cloud sync (Settings → Dictionary → Cloud
sync, off by default), the app periodically asks our sync server
whether a newer version of your installed language packs is available.
The HTTP request contains:

* The BCP-47 language code (e.g. `en`, `vi`) you have installed.
* The integer version cursor your local copy is currently on
  (so the server can reply with only the delta).
* A per-installation random identifier (`userId`), generated locally with
  `java.util.UUID.randomUUID()` on first launch, stored in
  `SharedPreferences`, and never associated with a name, email, or
  advertising ID. This identifier exists only so the server can flag
  corrupt installs (see §2.1.3) without us knowing who you are.
* The app's `User-Agent` header: `Lexicon/1.0 (Android)`.

The HTTP response contains the list of INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE word changes
since your cursor, and the URL of a `.db.zst` language-pack file on the
CDN if a full re-download is required. No word you have searched for, no
word you have saved, and no annotation you have made is ever uploaded.

#### 2.1.3 Integrity-check result

After each delta sync, the app runs `PRAGMA integrity_check` against the
local SQLite database and POSTs the text result (`ok` or the diagnostic
string) to `/api/v1/integrity/{userId}`. The diagnostic string contains
no user content — it is generated by SQLite's internal consistency
checker. This is best-effort telemetry: if the POST fails, the app
continues normally.

### 2.2 Data that *never* leaves the device

* **Search history** (typed and voice). Stored in `SharedPreferences`,
  never uploaded. Clearable from Settings → Privacy & Data → Clear recent
  searches.
* **Saved words & collections.** Stored in the local SQLite database
  (`lexicon.db`) under your app-private data directory. Never uploaded.
* **Voice-search audio.** Captured by the microphone, transcribed on-device
  by Android's `SpeechRecognizer`, and immediately discarded. The audio
  is never written to disk and never uploaded. Only the resulting text
  query is used — and that is treated identically to a typed search
  (§2.2 first bullet).
* **Image-search photos.** Captured by the camera, processed on-device
  by ML Kit text recognition, and immediately discarded. The image is
  never written to disk and never uploaded. Only the recognised text is
  used for lookup.
* **Theme, font, and reading-mode preferences.** Stored in
  `SharedPreferences`, never uploaded.
* **Crash counters and feature-usage flags.** Stored locally for the
  app's own use; not uploaded.

## 3. The cloud sync service

The optional cloud sync service is a Ktor application (see
`native/cloud/server/LexiconSyncServer.kt`) running on infrastructure
operated by Lexicon. It exposes three endpoints:

| Method | Path                                       | Purpose                                  |
|--------|-------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| GET    | `/api/v1/sync/{languageCode}?since={v}`   | Fetch word-list delta changes.           |
| GET    | `{cdnUrl}/<language>.db.zst`              | Download a full compressed language pack.|
| POST   | `/api/v1/integrity/{userId}`              | Report local SQLite integrity check.     |

All three endpoints are served over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). The server does not
log request bodies, does not write your IP address to persistent storage,
and does not share data with any third party. Server-side logs are
rotated daily and retained for no longer than 30 days for operational
debugging.

## 4. Permissions — and why each one is requested

The Play Store requires developers to justify every Android permission
their app requests. Lexicon requests **only three** runtime permissions.
None of them is mandatory to use the core dictionary; each one unlocks a
single optional feature and can be denied without breaking the app.

### 4.1 `android.permission.CAMERA`

* **Declared in:** `AndroidManifest.xml`
* **Runtime requested:** When the user taps the **📷 image search** button
  inside the Search modal.
* **Why:** To recognise a word printed in a book, on a sign, or in
  handwriting. The camera frame is processed on-device by ML Kit text
  recognition; the recognised text is then looked up in the local
  dictionary. No image data is written to disk and no image data is
  uploaded (see §2.2).
* **Denied?** Image search is unavailable. Typed and voice search
  continue to work.

### 4.2 `android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`

* **Declared in:** `AndroidManifest.xml`
* **Runtime requested:** When the user taps the **🎤 voice search** button
  inside the Search modal.
* **Why:** To capture a short utterance for on-device speech-to-text via
  Android's `SpeechRecognizer`. The audio buffer is held in memory for
  the duration of the recognition call (typically < 2 seconds) and then
  discarded. It is never written to disk and never uploaded (see §2.2).
* **Denied?** Voice search is unavailable. Typed and image search
  continue to work.

### 4.3 `android.permission.INTERNET`

* **Declared in:** `AndroidManifest.xml`
* **Runtime requested:** Not a runtime permission — granted at install.
* **Why:** Used **only** by the optional cloud sync client
  (`com.lexicon.engine.DeltaSyncClient`) when the user has opted in to
  cloud sync (Settings → Dictionary → Cloud sync, off by default). With
  sync off, the app makes zero network requests at runtime. The
  permission is still declared in the manifest because the sync code is
  shipped in every build; turning sync on must not require a re-install.
* **Denied / sync off?** The app is fully functional in offline mode.
  Language-pack updates simply will not be fetched.

### 4.4 Permissions we do **not** request

The following permissions are commonly requested by dictionary apps but
**not** requested by Lexicon. They are listed here explicitly so that the
Play Console's Data Safety form and reviewers can verify our claims:

| Permission                            | Why we don't need it                          |
|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` | No feature uses location. Region detection for language-pack defaults is done via `Locale`, not GPS. |
| `READ_CONTACTS` / `WRITE_CONTACTS`    | No social or contact-sharing feature.         |
| `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | All data is stored in app-private storage. We never read or write outside the app sandbox. |
| `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` / `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` | Image search uses the live camera frame, not the gallery. |
| `GET_ACCOUNTS` / `USE_CREDENTIALS`    | No account system. Sync uses a locally-generated random UUID. |
| `READ_PHONE_STATE`                    | No telephony integration.                     |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`                  | No push notifications are sent.               |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`              | No background service starts on boot.         |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`                  | Sync runs in `WorkManager` only while the app is in the foreground. |
| `BILLING`                             | No in-app purchases.                          |
| `AD_ID`                               | No ads.                                       |

## 5. How we share data

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for
advertising or commercial purposes. The only third parties that process
any data on our behalf are:

* **Crash reporting provider** — receives anonymised crash logs as
  described in §2.1.1. Acts as a data processor under our instructions.
* **Cloud sync hosting provider** — hosts the Ktor sync server described
  in §3. Receives only the sync metadata described in §2.1.2 and §2.1.3.

We will disclose data to law enforcement **only** if required by a valid
court order, and only to the extent the order compels. Because we do not
collect your search history, saved words, voice, or images, we have
nothing of yours to disclose even if served with such an order.

## 6. Data retention

| Data type              | Retention period                                                |
|------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Crash logs             | 90 days from receipt, then automatically deleted.              |
| Sync metadata          | Discarded as soon as the HTTP response is returned. Not stored server-side beyond request logs (30-day rotation). |
| Integrity-check result | Same as sync metadata.                                          |
| Local app data         | Retained on your device until you uninstall the app or use the in-app "Clear cache" / "Clear recent searches" controls. |

## 7. Children's privacy

Lexicon is a general-audience dictionary. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13. The Play Console "Target
audience" setting is 13+, and the content rating is "Everyone". Because
we collect no personal information from anyone — child or adult — the
COPPA / GDPR-K specific provisions do not materially apply, but the
default protections of this policy extend to all users.

---

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (EEA/UK GDPR, California CCPA, Brazilian
LGPD, etc.) you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export
your personal data. Because we collect no personal data linked to your
identity, most of these rights are already satisfied by uninstalling the
app — that deletes every byte we have stored about you, locally. For
server-side data (the crash log and the sync metadata described in
§2.1), contact <privacy@lexicon.app> and we will action your request
within 30 days.
