Fixed by: (a) swapping the old bucket vector out, (b) allocating a zero-initialised new vector (`assign(new_size, HashEntry{})`), (c) re-inserting every non-empty old entry via `robin_hood_insert` against the new bucket count. Also switched growth from linear-by-one to geometric (double until >= target) so amortised insertion stays O(1) instead of O(n^2); load-factor target (~0.7, i.e. `1/1.43`) is preserved.
